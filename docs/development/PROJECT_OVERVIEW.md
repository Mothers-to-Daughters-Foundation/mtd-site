# Project Overview

## Introduction

The Mothers to Daughters (M2D) Mentorship Platform is a web application designed to connect women seeking personal and professional growth with experienced mentors in a structured, secure, and scalable environment.

The platform enables administrators to manage mentorship relationships, subscriptions, educational resources, and user accounts while providing mentors and mentees with role-specific dashboards.

The application is built using modern web technologies and is designed to support future growth as the organization expands.

---

## Vision

To create a trusted digital mentorship platform that empowers women by making meaningful mentorship accessible regardless of location.

---

## Mission

The M2D platform exists to:

- Connect mentors with mentees.
- Simplify mentorship management.
- Provide educational resources.
- Support personal and professional development.
- Build lasting mentoring relationships.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional mentorship programs often struggle with:

- Manual mentor matching
- Poor communication
- Lack of centralized resources
- Difficulty tracking mentorship progress
- Subscription and payment management
- Administrative overhead

The M2D platform addresses these challenges through automation and centralized management.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives are to:

- Provide secure user authentication.
- Support multiple user roles.
- Enable mentor and mentee matching.
- Manage subscriptions.
- Track mentoring relationships.
- Deliver learning resources.
- Simplify administration.
- Create a scalable foundation for future features.

---

## Target Users

### Administrators

Administrators oversee the entire platform.

Responsibilities include:

- Managing users
- Creating subscription plans
- Assigning mentors
- Monitoring mentorships
- Managing resources
- Reviewing platform activity

---

### Mentors

Mentors provide guidance and support to assigned mentees.

Responsibilities include:

- Managing their profile
- Viewing assigned mentees
- Conducting mentorship sessions
- Sharing expertise
- Accessing mentoring resources

---

### Mentees

Mentees use the platform to receive mentorship.

Responsibilities include:

- Managing their profile
- Maintaining an active subscription
- Viewing mentor information
- Accessing resources
- Participating in mentorship sessions

## High-Level Workflow

1. User registers.
2. User profile is created.
3. Administrator reviews the account.
4. User subscribes to a plan.
5. Administrator assigns a mentor.
6. Mentor and mentee begin their mentorship relationship.
7. Resources and sessions are managed through the platform.
