## Accelerating Gender Equality: Key Discussions at the 69th Session of the UN Commission on the Status of Women

### Francine Mbvoumbou Represents Mothers to Daughters at the 69th UN Commission on the Status of Women

We are proud to announce that **Francine Mbvoumbou**, Founder & Strategic Leader of **Mothers to Daughters**, has been selected as a **delegate** for the **69th Session of the UN Commission on the Status of Women (CSW69)**.

This prestigious global event, organized by **UN Women**, gathers leaders, policymakers, and advocates to advance gender equality and women's empowerment worldwide. As a dedicated advocate, Francine Mbvoumbou continues to champion **mentorship, leadership, and opportunities** for young women and girls, ensuring their voices are heard on the international stage.

Stay connected with us as we share updates from CSW69 and the impactful contributions of **Mothers to Daughters** in shaping a more equitable future.
