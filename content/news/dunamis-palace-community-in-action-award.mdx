We are thrilled to announce that The Dunamis Palace, during its 2024 Annual Gala Night, honored Francine Mbvoumbo with the Community in Action Award. She was commended for her outstanding leadership and hands-on contributions to community-driven projects that continue to spark lasting positive impacts internationally. This remarkable recognition reflects Francine's unwavering commitment to uplift and empower, inspiring us all to broaden our reach and deepen our dedication to building stronger communities worldwide.
