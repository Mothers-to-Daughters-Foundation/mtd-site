Mothers to Daughters (M2D) has announced its expansion into the United States. The announcement coincides with the organization's fifth anniversary on March 8, 2025.

The expansion focuses on supporting women entrepreneurs through intergenerational mentorship and advancing gender equity. M2D has set an ambitious target of assisting 100,000 women-led businesses by 2035, demonstrating the organization's long-term commitment to economic empowerment.

The organization is rolling out several key initiatives as part of this expansion:

- Exclusive Fundraising Soirée in Manhattan
- Intergenerational Entrepreneurship Program
- PACE Festival in Toronto

Founder Francine Mbvoumbo's vision emphasizes helping women achieve financial independence through mentorship and entrepreneurial support. The organization is recognized as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
