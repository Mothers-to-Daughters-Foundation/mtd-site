Mothers to Daughters (M2D), a global nonprofit organization, officially launched its U.S. operations with an event held on Thursday, April 24th at the Microsoft Experience Center in Midtown Manhattan. The venue, located at 677 Fifth Avenue on the 7th floor, provided attendees with both sweeping city views and hands-on demonstrations of cutting-edge technology.

The evening featured immersive tours of the Experience Center alongside M2D's signature programming. The space symbolized "vision, elevation, and potential" while aligning with the organization's mission to prepare women for leadership roles in an evolving world.

The event brought together a cross-generational group of women and allies from various industries. Participants included entrepreneurs in their twenties alongside executives in their sixties, each sharing personal and professional journeys. Francine Mbvoumbo, founder of Mothers to Daughters, characterized the occasion as "more than a launch—it was a representation of our mission in motion."

The gathering emphasized mentorship and women's leadership development across diverse age groups and professional backgrounds, marking a significant milestone for the organization's expansion into the United States.
