We had an incredible photoshoot in Toronto for our Billboard feature at Dundas Square and Exhibition Place, which was showcased from February 18-28 and again on March 8 in celebration of International Women’s Day and our 5th anniversary. The shoot was a collaborative effort with an amazing team and community members, and by all accounts, it was a tremendous success. We are deeply grateful to our partners—[Branded Cities](https://www.brandedcities.ca/), [Mint Room Studios](https://click.convertkit-mail2.com/8ku7gexvegtoh22k4zebkhkdo8999s3/3ohphkh36q46qpbr/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cubWludHJvb20uY2Ev), [Style & Image Consultant Anna Lolomari](https://click.convertkit-mail2.com/8ku7gexvegtoh22k4zebkhkdo8999s3/58hvh7hgp5kp5xs6/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuemVzdHlsaWZlc3R5bGUuY2Ev), [Assistant Stylist Ariana Marques](http://arianamarques.com/), [Lead Photographer Preston Farrell](https://www.pressthebest.ca/preston), [2D Artist Lin Cao](https://lincaoart.wixsite.com/lincao) and Maya Nasiri—for their invaluable contributions in bringing this vision to life.
