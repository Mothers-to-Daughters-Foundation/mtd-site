M2D organized a photoshoot in Toronto featuring billboard displays at Dundas Square and Exhibition Place. The campaign ran from February 18-28, with an additional showing on March 8 to commemorate International Women's Day and the organization's fifth anniversary.

The project brought together a collaborative team of creative professionals. The organization expressed gratitude to multiple partners who contributed their expertise, including Branded Cities, Mint Room Studios, styling consultant Anna Lolomari, assistant stylist Ariana Marques, lead photographer Preston Farrell, 2D artist Lin Cao, and Maya Nasiri. According to the announcement, "The shoot was a collaborative effort with an amazing team and community members, and by all accounts, it was a tremendous success."
