We are excited to share that Mothers to Daughters (M2D) has been invited to participate in **International Cooperation Futures**, an event uniting pioneering leaders and organizations dedicated to shaping a more equitable global community. During this gathering, M2D will join in thought-provoking discussions, innovative collaborations, and knowledge-sharing sessions aimed at driving lasting social impact worldwide. This invitation underscores M2D’s commitment to empowering women, strengthening communities, and fostering sustainable partnerships on an international scale.
