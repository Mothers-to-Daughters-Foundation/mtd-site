# Applications Open for the Intergenerational Mentoring Program

Applications are now open for the M2D Intergenerational Mentoring Program—a
high-impact, six-month journey designed to equip young women with the
entrepreneurial mindset, strategies, and leadership skills they need to excel.

Through immersive mentorship and hands-on workshops, participants build the
confidence and connections to thrive—at no cost to them.

- **5+** years empowering women
- **100+** mentorship pairs formed
- **200+** hybrid networking events since 2020
- **50,000+** online engagements

Ready to take part as a mentor or mentee? Complete our interest form and our
team will be in touch.
