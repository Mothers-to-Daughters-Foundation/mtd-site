Mothers to Daughters has received an invitation to participate in the upcoming UN Summit of the Future. This international gathering will convene heads of state, thought leaders, and civil society partners to establish a collective global vision. M2D's participation reflects the organization's dedication to promoting women's empowerment, facilitating conversations across generations, and developing sustainable initiatives that benefit communities worldwide.
