We are delighted to announce that Mothers to Daughters has been invited to participate in the upcoming [UN Summit of the Future](https://www.un.org/en/summit-of-the-future). This significant international gathering will bring together heads of state, thought leaders, and civil society partners to shape a shared global vision. M2D’s involvement underscores our commitment to advocating for women’s empowerment, intergenerational dialogue, and sustainable solutions that uplift communities around the world.
