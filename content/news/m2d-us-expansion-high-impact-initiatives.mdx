Mothers to Daughters, a Canadian not-for-profit organization, announced a significant expansion into the United States. The organization, which operates through its U.S. parent company Mothers to Daughters Inc. (501(c)(3)), aims to strengthen its mission of fostering intergenerational mentorship and supporting women entrepreneurs.

The expansion announcement coincides with the organization's fifth anniversary on March 8, 2025. Since its founding, Mothers to Daughters has built a community of more than 3,000 women across North America through its generational-insight-led approach, which connects emerging leaders with experienced women entrepreneurs who share relevant expertise and lived experiences.

A central objective driving this expansion is an ambitious goal to support 100,000 women-led businesses by 2035, thereby promoting women's lasting financial independence through entrepreneurship.

According to Francine Mbvoumbo, Founder and President, the organization's approach leverages the power of mentorship across generations. She stated that by expanding reach, "we're building a future where women's economic independence is strengthened through entrepreneurship and mentorship," and emphasized that the 2035 target, while ambitious, remains achievable through collaborative efforts.

The expansion represents Mothers to Daughters' continued commitment to advancing gender equity and economic empowerment through strategic growth initiatives.
