Francine is honored to be attending the 69th session of the Commission on the Status of Women at the United Nations in NYC from March 10–21, as a delegate of SAEF. This year is especially significant as the global community marks 30 years since the Fourth World Conference on Women and the adoption of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action (1995)—a landmark commitment to advancing gender equality.

This session brings together representatives from governments, UN entities, and NGOs worldwide to assess progress, address challenges, and drive meaningful action for women’s rights. We are proud to have her there and contribute to these critical conversations!
