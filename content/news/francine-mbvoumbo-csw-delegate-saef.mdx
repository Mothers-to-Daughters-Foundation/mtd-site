Francine has been appointed to attend the 69th session of the Commission on the Status of Women at the United Nations in New York from March 10–21 as a delegate of the Southern Africa Embrace Foundation (SAEF).

This year marks a significant milestone, as the global community marks 30 years since the Fourth World Conference on Women and the adoption of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action (1995).

The session convenes representatives from governments, UN entities, and NGOs globally to evaluate advancements, identify obstacles, and promote substantive progress on women's rights. Mothers to Daughters expressed pride in having Francine participate in these consequential discussions affecting women worldwide.
