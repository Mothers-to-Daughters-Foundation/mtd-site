We are thrilled to share that Francine Mbvoumbo, founder of Mothers to Daughters (M2D), was recently featured on the *Breaking Brave with Marilyn Barefoot* podcast. In this insightful episode, Francine explores the vital importance of empowering women and girls as a cornerstone for building more resilient communities. Drawing from her personal experiences and M2D's core mission, she highlights how mentorship and collective action can drive lasting, transformative change across generations.
