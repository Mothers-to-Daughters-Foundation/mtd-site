Mothers to Daughters (M2D) is delighted to be featured in the article *Bridging the Generation Gap* by *Living Luxe*. The piece explores how M2D's intergenerational approach fosters meaningful connections, mentorship, and shared empowerment among women of all ages. Through real-life success stories and impactful initiatives, *Bridging the Generation Gap* shines a light on M2D's commitment to uplifting communities by uniting women across generations.
