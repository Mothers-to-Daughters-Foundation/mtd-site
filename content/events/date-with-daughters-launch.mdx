# Date With Daughters Launch (Women Only)

## About the Event

An intimate dinner gathering designed as a launch event for the Date with Daughters initiative. The evening emphasizes meaningful connection: one night, one table, real introductions.

## Why This Matters

Date with Daughters is a signature program by Mothers to Daughters (M2D), originally launched virtually in 2020 and now returning as an in-person dinner series based in New York City. The initiative creates a trusted environment where women connect for mentorship, professional introductions, and collaborative growth. The organization also welcomes male allies as part of the broader ecosystem supporting this mission.

The program envisions expanding globally to bring together women for meaningful professional and personal development opportunities.
