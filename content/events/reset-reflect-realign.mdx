# M2D Workshop: Reset, Reflect, Realign: A 3-Step Yogic Journey to Your Ideal Self

## About the Event

As the year draws to a close, join [Ankita Rao](https://www.linkedin.com/in/raoankita/) for a transformative 60-minute workshop that blends reflection with action.

- **Uncover internal blockers** and identify practical ways to move forward with perspective and self-assuredness
- **Reconnect with your Ideal Self** and gain clarity on who you are at your best
- **Embody your Ideal Self** across business and life, grounded in **samādhi** — a liberated way of being

**About Ankita Rao**

[Ankita](https://www.linkedin.com/in/raoankita/) [Rao](https://www.linkedin.com/in/raoankita/) is the founder of [Lost and Found Transformation](https://www.lostandfoundtransformation.com/), a platform dedicated to helping individuals navigate personal and professional growth through mindfulness and self-awareness. With a background in yoga and a passion for transformative practices, Ankita empowers individuals to reconnect with their authentic selves and lead fulfilling lives.

**About Mothers to Daughters (M2D)**

Mothers to Daughters is a *for-impact global community* dedicated to empowering women economically, intellectually, and socially through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development.

Our mission is to equip women to lead, thrive, and build lasting legacies — ensuring that women have equal opportunities to shape the decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Our vision is bold: to support the launch and growth of 100,000 women-owned businesses by 2035, creating a ripple effect of empowerment and global impact.
