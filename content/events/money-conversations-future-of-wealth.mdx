# Money Conversations: Mentorship & the Future of Wealth

## About the Event

This gathering brings together emerging entrepreneurs for structured dialogue around wealth-building and generational impact. The conversation centers on how mentorship, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship form the foundation of lasting family legacies.

The event explores the connection between financial literacy becoming confidence, entrepreneurship becoming leverage, and intentional wealth creation across generations. Light refreshments will be provided.

## Featured Speaker

**Constance Victory** is a New York City-based design entrepreneur and real estate strategist. She holds degrees from Rhode Island School of Design and NYU/UCL. Her accessories have been stocked by Free People and the Brooklyn Museum, and she received recognition from India's Ministry of Commerce & Industry for design excellence. She regularly leads discussions on entrepreneurship, leadership, and financial literacy.

## Organizers

**Mothers to Daughters (M2D)** is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit advancing women's economic empowerment through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development, with a goal of supporting 100,000 women-led initiatives by 2035.

**New York Life** partners on the event, offering financial education and strategic planning support.
