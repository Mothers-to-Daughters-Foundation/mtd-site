# Careers Are Assets: Negotiating Pivotal Moments with Strategy & Confidence

## About the Webinar

This session examines how career decisions are made in practice — pay, promotions, performance narratives, and exits — enabling mentors and mentees to move forward strategically rather than rely on assumptions.

The webinar provides insider knowledge on the decision-making processes that shape professional trajectories, helping participants approach pivotal career moments with greater confidence and intentionality.
