# Ball of Wisdom: A Black Tie Leadership & Legacy Evening

## Overview

A curated invitation-only soirée hosted by Mothers to Daughters (M2D) marking the organization's first anniversary. The event brings together founders, executives, and investors across industries for meaningful connection and long-term impact, held at Renaissance New York Chelsea Hotel.

## The Evening

**Timing & Dress Code**
- Black tie required
- Private cocktail reception: 6–7pm
- General admission access begins: 6:30pm
- Carefully curated guest list

**Program Components**
- Keynote moments and curated programming
- Ball of Wisdom Awards honoring women driving intergenerational impact
- Silent auction featuring art, experiences, and collectible pieces
- Entertainment: jazz performances, multimedia poetry, and Broadway musicians
- Mother-daughter storytelling and mentor-mentee testimonials
- Facilitated introductions for high-value connections
- Live painting

## Who Attends

Intentionally selected guests including founders and entrepreneurs, investors and capital allocators, C-suite executives, and leaders across technology, fashion, media, and policy sectors.

## Mission & Impact

M2D is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit operating at the intersection of capital, culture, and community. This fundraising event supports the organization's mission to advance women's economic empowerment through intergenerational mentorship, leadership development, and access to opportunity, with a commitment to supporting 100,000 women-led initiatives by 2035.
