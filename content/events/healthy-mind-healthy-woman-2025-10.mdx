# M2D Workshop: Healthy Mind, Healthy Woman

## About the Event

Join us for an inspiring and interactive session with [Anna Ananiandis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/annamaria-ananiadis-46500714/)!

In this session, Anna will share her personal journey and insights on navigating the challenges that many aspiring leaders face — both personally and professionally.

## Through this conversation, we’ll explore how to:

- Handle difficult conversations with confidence and empathy
- Build resilience and foster emotional well-being
- Practice self-care and sustain motivation in high-pressure environments

This session is designed to empower you with practical strategies to thrive — not just succeed — in your leadership journey.

## About the Speaker

[Annamaria Ananiandis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/annamaria-ananiadis-46500714/) is an accomplished leader and mentor with experience with the Federal Public Service of Canada. Her practical wisdom and compassionate approach continue to inspire emerging leaders around the world.

## About Mothers to Daughters (M2D)

Mothers to Daughters is a *for-impact global community* dedicated to empowering women economically, intellectually, and socially through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development.

Our mission is to equip women to lead, thrive, and build lasting legacies — ensuring that women have equal opportunities to shape the decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Our vision is bold: to support the launch and growth of 100,000 women-owned businesses by 2035, creating a ripple effect of empowerment and global impact.
