# How to Play with the Unknown & Be a Master Innovator

## About the Event

We’re honored to welcome Michele Risa, visionary leader, TEDx speaker, and co-author of Conscious Entrepreneurs, to the Art of Becoming Series.

With a background in psychology, ancient wisdom, and business strategy, Michele has spent decades helping changemakers embody authenticity, resilience, and purpose. From the United Nations to healing circles across the globe, her voice is one of courage, connection, and conscious leadership.

Join us for this intimate conversation as we explore how to lead from within, live intentionally, and grow through life's most pivotal transitions. This is more than a talk—it’s a reminder that we were never meant to journey alone.
