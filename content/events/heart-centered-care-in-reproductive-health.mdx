# M2D Workshop: Heart-Centered Care in Reproductive Health

## About the Event

**Join us for an inspiring and interactive session with** [**Dr. Mary Sabo!**](https://www.instagram.com/lilyandhorn/)

In this session, [Dr. Sabo](https://www.instagram.com/lilyandhorn/) will share her journey and insights on navigating the challenges many aspiring leaders face — personally and professionally — through the lens of **Heart-Centered Care in Reproductive Health**.

**Through this conversation, you’ll explore how to:**

- Approach reproductive health with empathy and informed decision-making
- Build resilience and emotional well-being during challenging journeys
- Integrate both modern and holistic self-care practices that support long-term health and peace of mind

This session is designed to empower you with practical strategies to thrive — not just succeed — in your personal and professional life.

**About Dr. Mary Sabo**

[Dr. Mary Sabo](https://www.instagram.com/lilyandhorn/) is the founder of [**Lily + Horn**](https://www.lilyandhorn.com/), an integrative reproductive health clinic in New York City. She combines modern medicine with holistic therapies like acupuncture, nutrition, and mental health support to guide patients toward healthier outcomes and greater peace of mind. Her compassionate, patient-centered approach inspires women to feel empowered throughout their reproductive journeys.

## About Mothers to Daughters (M2D)

Mothers to Daughters is a *for-impact global community* dedicated to empowering women economically, intellectually, and socially through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development.

Our mission is to equip women to lead, thrive, and build lasting legacies — ensuring that women have equal opportunities to shape the decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Our vision is bold: to support the launch and growth of 100,000 women-owned businesses by 2035, creating a ripple effect of empowerment and global impact.
