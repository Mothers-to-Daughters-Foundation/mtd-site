# M2D Workshop: Heart-Centered Care in Reproductive Health

## About the Event

Join us for an interactive session with Dr. Mary Sabo, part of The Art of Building Series.

Dr. Mary Sabo leads Lily + Horn with the belief that every reproductive journey deserves care that is personal, integrative, and nurturing. Her team supports patients with both modern medicine and holistic therapies—guiding them toward healthier outcomes and greater peace of mind.
