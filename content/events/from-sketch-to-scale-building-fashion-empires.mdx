# M2D Powerhouse Panel: From Sketch to Scale – Building Fashion Empires

## About the Event

Join us for an inspiring conversation with trailblazing fashion leaders who have transformed their passions into global brands. These women share a common thread: fashion is the fabric of their lives — its vision, identity, and grit. In this session, they reveal how they started small, thought big, and scaled their dreams into lasting legacies.

**Meet the Panelists**

**Kibonen Nfi**

Founder & CEO, Kibonen NY

Kibonen Nfi is a Cameroonian fashion designer who reimagines traditional African garments through a contemporary lens. Drawing inspiration from her heritage and New York's vibrant fashion scene, she has showcased her work at Mercedes-Benz Fashion Week and was featured in Vogue Italia's 2015 Talents edition. [Kibonen](https://kibonen.com/pages/about?srsltid=AfmBOor7BM9rd_1sShMm182qK2VNqMX6ixkcyt_trSWKcQ5jp_m5tEk1&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Lara Tyler**

Co-Founder, Jolana NYC

Lara Tyler, along with her daughter Olivia, founded Jolana NYC to create luxury handbags that embody the glamour and grit of New York City. Their designs celebrate slow fashion and the artistry of small-batch production. [Jolana NYC](https://jolananyc.com/)

**Samantha Siu**

Founder, Samantha Siu Jewelry

Raised in a family of jewelry manufacturers, Samantha Siu founded her eponymous brand to create fine jewelry that tells a story. Her designs have been featured in Vogue and are known for their ethical craftsmanship and bold aesthetics. [Samantha Siu - New York](https://samanthasiu.com/pages/ourstorymission?srsltid=AfmBOoqnVrr8yQZaxxP5wXGBPpq8VQp6o9Oobu7icARdArpAiuI9vcP0&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Michelle Akers**

Founder & Designer, Live in Fashion

Michelle Akers transitioned from a successful career in fashion retail to launch Live in Fashion, an online boutique that empowers women through stylish and functional designs. Her journey reflects resilience and a commitment to female entrepreneurship. [LIVEINFASHION.COM](http://LIVEINFASHION.COM)

**Dani Kagan & Victoria Marshman**

Co-Founders, Mave & Chez

Dani Kagan and Victoria Marshman, best friends and serial entrepreneurs, co-founded Mave & Chez to revolutionize women's footwear. Their ergonomic slippers blend comfort with style, addressing the needs of modern women. [Mave and Chez](https://maveandchez.com/en-us/pages/about?srsltid=AfmBOoqRnnqPBmYQDUMdd5EJYYxJgsoKITsZym-4Ejv7imt59mrLSCT7&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**About Mothers to Daughters (M2D)**

Mothers to Daughters is a *for-impact global community* dedicated to empowering women economically, intellectually, and socially through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development.

Our mission is to equip women to lead, thrive, and build lasting legacies — ensuring that women have equal opportunities to shape the decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Our vision is bold: to support the launch and growth of 100,000 women-owned businesses by 2035, creating a ripple effect of empowerment and global impact.
