# M2D Workshop: Building People-First Startups with Alexis

## About the Event

**Join us for an inspiring and interactive session with** [**Alexis Prieto!**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexisprietostartuphr/)

In this session, Alexis will share her journey and insights on navigating the challenges many startup leaders face — personally and professionally — through the lens of **Building People-First Startups**.

**Through this conversation, you’ll explore how to:**

- Build a strong, purpose-driven team culture from day one
- Navigate HR challenges to empower employees and drive growth
- Foster leadership practices that prioritize people and performance

This session is designed to equip you with practical strategies to create thriving startups where people — and culture — are a source of strength and possibility.

**About Alexis Prieto**

Alexis Prieto founded [**Startup HR Talent**](https://startuphrandtalent.com/) with a simple belief: every great startup deserves the right people and culture to grow. Inspired by the challenges founders face in scaling teams, Alexis built a platform to help leaders turn HR into a source of strength, purpose, and possibility — empowering startups to grow with people at the heart of their success.

**🌍 About Mothers to Daughters (M2D)**

Mothers to Daughters is a *for-impact global community* dedicated to empowering women economically, intellectually, and socially through intergenerational mentorship and leadership development.

Our mission is to equip women to lead, thrive, and build lasting legacies — ensuring that women have equal opportunities to shape the decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Our vision is bold: to support the launch and growth of 100,000 women-owned businesses by 2035, creating a ripple effect of empowerment and global impact.
