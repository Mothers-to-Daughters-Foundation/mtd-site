# The Mothers to Daughters (M2D) Legacy Walk

## About the Event

The M2D Legacy Walk is Mothers to Daughters' signature impact runway experience celebrating the powerful bond between biological and spiritual mothers and daughters.

This celebration honors the legacy of women across generations. The event brings together mothers and daughters — both biological and spiritual relationships — to recognize and commemorate their interconnected journeys and impact.
