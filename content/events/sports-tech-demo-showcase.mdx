# Sports Tech Demo Showcase

## About the Event

We’re proud to announce that Mothers to Daughters (M2D) will be spotlighted as the featured charity at this exclusive NBA Summer League event, where innovation meets impact. As we showcase the most advanced breakthroughs in athletic training, recovery, and performance, we’re also shining a light on the critical need for women’s economic empowerment—across all fields, including sports. This isn’t just a demo. It’s a movement—where elite performance and purpose-driven progress go hand in hand.

This is your invitation to witness the most advanced innovations in athletic training, recovery, and performance enhancement — all in one unforgettable showcase. Designed for NBA trainers, athletes, and team stakeholders, this demo blends live athlete testing, real-time data, and breakthrough tools that elevate the standard of performance and recovery.
