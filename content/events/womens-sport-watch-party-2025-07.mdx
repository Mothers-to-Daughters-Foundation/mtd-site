# Women’s Sport Watch Party

## About the Event

This powerful collaboration between Schmooze and Mothers to Daughters will bring together dynamic women executive and entrepreneurs from across industries for an evening dedicated to raising awareness around women’s sports, celebrating entrepreneurship, and fostering authentic community building in the heart of New York’s Financial District.
