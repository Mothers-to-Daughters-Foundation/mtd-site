At Mothers to Daughters, we cultivate powerful relationships where Mothers
(Mentors) equip Daughters (Mentees) with the tools to thrive socially and
economically. In turn, Daughters honor this wisdom by applying it in their
lives and passing it to future generations—ensuring its impact extends far
beyond today.

Intergenerational mentoring is more than guidance; it's a mindset that sparks
real change. A strong support system has guided me through my own challenges,
and I'm committed to sharing its benefits with as many women as we can reach.

## The Ripple Effect

When one woman is equipped to lead, she doesn't rise alone. She lifts her
family, her community, and the next generation of women behind her. That ripple
is the heart of everything we do—and it's why we're building a global movement
dedicated to forging deep connections, amplifying voices, and driving
meaningful action.

*— Francine Mbvoumbo, Chair & President*
