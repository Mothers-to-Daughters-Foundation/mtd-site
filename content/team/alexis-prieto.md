Alexis Prieto is a passionate people leader with over a decade of experience in Human Resources, People Operations, and Talent Acquisition. As the Founder of Startup HR, a woman-owned and Latina-owned consulting firm, Alexis partners with early-stage startups to optimize their workforce and build human-centered, inclusive organizations. Her expertise lies in developing HR strategies that drive growth, enhance culture, and promote diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging (DEIB).

In addition to her consulting work, Alexis serves as a Board Member for Women in Tech & Entrepreneurship, where she supports the growth and advancement of women in the tech industry through workshops, networking sessions, and mentoring opportunities. She is deeply committed to empowering individuals to maximize their impact and is passionate about fostering positive, inclusive workplace cultures that prioritize collaboration, innovation, and diversity. Alexis' dedication to driving change makes her a trusted partner for organizations looking to build strong, people-focused foundations.

Alexis is known for her ability to attract and develop high-performing, resilient teams, and she excels in navigating the unique challenges of high-growth, technical environments. With a focus on aligning people with business objectives, she helps organizations create positive work environments where team members thrive and contribute to long-term success.
