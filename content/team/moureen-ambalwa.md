Moureen is a passionate entrepreneur and dedicated mother of two, proudly guiding young females toward a brighter future through the power of mentorship. As a strong African woman, Moureen believes in the importance of instilling values and confidence in the next generation, empowering them to embrace their potential and lead with purpose.

As the owner of a successful medical esthetics spa, Moureen combines her expertise in beauty and wellness with her commitment to community service. She strives to create an environment where young women can learn not only about the esthetics industry but also about self-care, resilience, and personal growth.

Through her involvement in Mothers to Daughters, Moureen is determined to make a positive impact, bridging the gap between knowledge and opportunity. She is dedicated to nurturing talent, inspiring ambition, and building a supportive network that fosters the dreams and aspirations of young females everywhere. Join Moureen as she works to make the world a better place, one mentorship at a time.
