Hanna, a newcomer from Ukraine, brings over 11 years of professional experience in education and customer service. With a successful track record of launching more than 120 educational courses across Europe, Asia, and North America, she is a seasoned expert in her field.

As a proud member of the Mothers to Daughters Team, Hanna is dedicated to supporting women and fostering a community driven by purpose.

She believes in the power of building relationships, growing confidence, and providing unwavering support. Hanna is committed to empowering women to overcome obstacles, pursue their goals, and dream bigger. A former ballet dancer and lifelong ballet enthusiast, Hanna combines her artistic passion with her professional endeavors.
