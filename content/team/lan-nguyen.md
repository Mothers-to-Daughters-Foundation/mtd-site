Lan Nguyen is a communications and digital content specialist with international experience across Europe, Africa, and Asia. She began her career in Paris, where she led internal communications for ITL France’s Africa & Indian Ocean cluster, spearheading a major digital transition and strengthening employee engagement across 11 markets.

She later joined Ajinomoto Foods Europe, collaborating with senior executives to enhance alignment across the EMEA region, redesign corporate digital platforms, and expand external visibility through high-impact SEO and social media initiatives. Now based in New York, Lan brings her communication and marketing expertise to both a growing start-up and Mothers to Daughters.

She specializes in creating content that is visually compelling, strategically crafted, and designed to inspire engagement.
