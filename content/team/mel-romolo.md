With over two decades of experience in entrepreneurship, Mel Romolo brings a wealth of strategic and human insight to her role as Advisor to the Chair. A seasoned business coach and trusted advisor, she has dedicated her career to helping entrepreneurs move from overwhelm to clarity by building smarter systems, stronger strategies, and sustainable balance.

Throughout her journey across accounting, taxation, business advisory, market research, and digital consulting, Mel has cultivated a deep understanding of what it takes to lead with purpose and authenticity. Her belief in women’s empowerment and the power of intergenerational wisdom continues to guide her work—reminding women entrepreneurs that success is not only about growth, but also about balance, legacy, and connection.

Formerly serving as Vice President of Mothers to Daughters, Mel has been instrumental in shaping the organization’s mentorship and leadership development initiatives. In her current capacity as Advisor to the Chair, she continues to champion the advancement of women worldwide, empowering them to build thriving businesses and meaningful lives rooted in confidence, collaboration, and care.
