Shefa has been a social sciences researcher with over seven years of experience in socio-economic and governance aspects. She has a demonstrated knowledge of managing and coordinating research projects and programs in the area of governance, local government, and the 2030 agenda for sustainable development goals.

She is currently working for the Federal Government of Canada as a collections officer. She possesses excellent administration, business communications, and necessary IT skills to negotiate and deal tactfully with an array of people to perform debt recovery actions for Government Programs. She holds a Master of Social Sciences from the University of Dhaka in Public Administration.

Shefa feels women's empowerment is critical to increasing the sense of self-worth for all women, no matter how developed a country is. While Shefa enjoys working towards this cause that provides her with mental rewards, she also loves to spend quality time with her family, read books, watch movies, and travel.
