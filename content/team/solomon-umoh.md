Solomon Umoh is a seasoned Web Developer, UIUX Designer and Digital Marketing expert with over seven years of experience in building and amplifying online presences. As the Founder and Director of Microsavvy Technologies Inc., he partners with both small businesses and large enterprises to shape their brand identity, optimize market positioning, and deliver tailored guidance that drives measurable growth.

At Microsavvy, Solomon combines technical craftsmanship with strategic insight—developing responsive websites on Wix, WordPress, Custom-made sites using HTML, CSS, Javascript, and Bootstrap, crafting SEO-driven content, and deploying targeted advertising campaigns that connect brands with their audiences. He is passionate about aligning a client’s digital experience with their broader mission, whether it's establishing credibility, boosting engagement, or scaling operations.

Known for his hands-on approach and commitment to clarity, Solomon provides professional and actionable recommendations that make digital transformation feel achievable and impactful. His goal is simple: help organizations create meaningful, lasting connections online—supported by strategic design and solid performance.
