Peter Frank is a Partner at Schulman Lobel Advisors, LLC in New York, bringing over 40 years of experience advising individuals and businesses on accounting, tax, and strategic growth. His expertise spans entertainment, real estate, and professional services, with a focus on helping both U.S. and international entrepreneurs navigate complex business landscapes.

As a father of two daughters, Peter deeply values the power of mentorship and intergenerational connection—principles that align closely with Mothers to Daughters’ mission of empowering women through leadership, mentorship, and economic advancement. In his advisory role, he brings a wealth of strategic insight to strengthen the organization’s U.S. presence and global partnerships, reflecting his lifelong commitment to purpose-driven growth and community impact.
