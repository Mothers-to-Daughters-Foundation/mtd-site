Ayo Ogunrinde is a dynamic professional with a strong background in engineering, project management, and business analysis, now channeling her creativity and leadership into digital storytelling and community engagement. Ayo brings a passion for amplifying voices, fostering intergenerational connections, and building meaningful online communities.

With over a decade of experience in the oil and gas sector and advanced studies in Project Management at Northeastern University, Ayo combines analytical thinking with a keen eye for creativity.

Ayo is dedicated to using her diverse skill set to highlight the inspiring stories within the Mothers to Daughters community, strengthen its digital presence, and empower women to connect, mentor, and thrive.
